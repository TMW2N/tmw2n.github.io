# Privacy Policy for AquaTestLog

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

**AquaTestLog** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the **AquaTestLog** mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Summary: Zero Data Collection
**AquaTestLog** operates entirely offline and locally on your device. 
* **We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.**
* **We do not require you to create an account or log in.**
* **We do not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.**
* **We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.**

## 2. Local Data Storage (Aquarium & Testing Logs)
All data you enter into the App—including aquarium profiles, livestock records, dosing notes, maintenance schedules, and water parameter test results (such as pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, alkalinity, salinity, and temperature)—is stored **exclusively on your local device**.

* **Your Control:** You have complete control over your data. We have no server access to view, recover, or modify your aquarium logs.
* **Data Deletion & Backups:** Because all data resides locally on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage will permanently delete your aquarium logs, unless you have independently backed up your device (such as through Apple iCloud Device Backups or Google Drive Device Backups managed by your operating system) or exported your data manually using the App's local export features.

## 3. Device Permissions
Depending on how you use the App, it may request access to certain features on your device. These permissions are used strictly for local functionality:
* **Notifications (Optional):** If enabled, the App uses local device notifications to remind you of scheduled water tests or tank maintenance. These schedules never leave your device.
* **File Storage (Optional):**  Used only when you choose to manually export or import a local backup file (e.g., CSV or JSON) of your water test history.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party data processors, cloud databases, or advertising platforms. 

*(Note: Your device's operating system provider, such as Apple Inc. or Google LLC, may independently collect standard diagnostic or crash data if you have opted into sharing analytics with them in your device's system settings. That data is governed by Apple's or Google's respective privacy policies.)*

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding children's data.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we update the App in the future in a way that changes how data is handled, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
