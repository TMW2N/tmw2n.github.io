Effective Date: October 18, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 18, 2026

SleepSoundly ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the SleepSoundly mobile application (the "App").

1. Summary: Zero Data Collection

SleepSoundly operates entirely offline and locally on your device.

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

All data you enter into the App is stored exclusively on your local device.

That data is governed by Apple's or Google's respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding children's data.
